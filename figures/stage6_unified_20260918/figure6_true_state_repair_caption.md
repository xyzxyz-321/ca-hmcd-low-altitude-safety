# Figure 6 caption

**Figure 6. True-state repair restores feasibility under perception
disturbance with an explicit immediate-service trade-off.** **a**, RepairOn
minus RepairOff feasibility. At noise 0.30, the gain was 0.7944 (95% BCa CI
0.7444 to 0.8444; Holm-adjusted P = 6.56e-6). **b**, The independent service
endpoint decreased by 0.0240 (-0.0386 to -0.0106; Holm-adjusted P = 0.00585)
at the same disturbance level. **c**, The complete true-state objective favored
the feasible repaired allocation by 0.4444 (0.4025 to 0.4897;
Holm-adjusted P = 7.30e-6). Points and whiskers are paired means and 95% BCa
intervals over 30 complete seed trajectories. The result validates the
implemented repair mechanism under the registered disturbance, not a universal
safety guarantee.
