# Figure 4 caption

**Figure 4. Controlled validation separates complementarity gains from
stability trade-offs.** **a,b**, CA-HMCD minus No-Synergy across the registered
complementarity scale in the public-event scenario. Complementarity produced a
monotone service-level gain, reaching 0.0049 (95% BCa CI 0.0033 to 0.0070;
Holm-adjusted P = 7.39e-5) at scale 2, whereas the corresponding complete-
objective difference was 0.0074 (-0.0026 to 0.0196; Holm-adjusted P = 0.731).
**c,d**, CA-HMCD minus No-Stability across state-volatility levels in the urban
corridor. At volatility 0.30, stability reduced switching by 3.46 events
(-3.85 to -3.05; Holm-adjusted P = 9.00e-6) while decreasing the immediate
service endpoint by 0.0205 (-0.0256 to -0.0156; Holm-adjusted P = 1.80e-5).
Points are paired means and whiskers are 95% BCa intervals over 30 complete
seed trajectories. Rings identify Holm-adjusted P < 0.05. The evidence supports
context-dependent complementarity and a deliberate stability-service trade-off,
not universal complete-objective superiority.
