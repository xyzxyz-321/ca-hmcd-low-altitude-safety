# Figure 3 caption

**Figure 3. Public-data-informed replay performance across task loads.** The
figure retains the registered UZH-FPV single-target and Anti-UAV410 composed
workload results because this evidence layer is unchanged by the later
mechanism and solver-boundary experiments. Independent units are public
trajectories or preregistered composed workloads, with 30 stochastic seeds
nested within each unit. Points show cluster means and uncertainty is computed
over independent workload units. The external service endpoint is
solver-decoupled and model-consistent. These task-side public data do not
constitute response-system field validation.
